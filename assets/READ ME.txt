YAP - free, private voice dictation for Windows
================================================

Hold Ctrl + Windows key, talk, let go. What you said is typed wherever your cursor is,
cleaned up: no "um"s or "uh"s, self-corrections fixed ("Tuesday, no wait, Wednesday"
becomes "Wednesday"), punctuation added, spoken lists turned into bullet points.

Everything runs on your own PC. Nothing you say is sent to the internet, and no account is needed.


INSTALLING
1. If you got Yap-Setup-Offline.zip, extract it first (right-click > Extract All)
   and run Yap-Setup.exe from the extracted folder, so it can find the models next to it.
   If you got just Yap-Setup.exe, run it.
2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway"
   (the installer is not code-signed).
3. Click through the installer. It doesn't need admin rights.
   Leave "Start Yap when I sign in" ticked so it is always ready.
4. Yap starts by itself. With just Yap-Setup.exe, the first start downloads the AI
   models (about 2 GB, or 3.5 GB with an NVIDIA graphics card) with a progress window.
5. Wait for the small "Yap is ready" message at the bottom of the screen.
6. Click into any text box, hold Ctrl + Windows, speak, and let go.

Yap is in the Start menu, and its icon (a microphone) sits near the clock; it may be
behind the ^ arrow. Right-click the icon for settings.

To remove it: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Yap > Uninstall.


TIPS
- Wrong microphone? Tray icon > Microphone.
- Say "new paragraph" or "new line" for line breaks.
- Pressing another key while holding Ctrl + Windows cancels the recording.
- Text went to the wrong window? Tray icon > Copy last dictation.
- Settings, history and logs are in C:\Users\<you>\.yap (tray icon > Edit settings / Open log).


WHAT YOUR PC NEEDS
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, about 5 GB of free disk space.
- Best with an NVIDIA graphics card (about 3 GB of video memory).
  Without one it still works: speech-to-text runs on the processor with a smaller model,
  which is slower and a little less accurate.
- Allow microphone access for desktop apps:
  Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone.
